# e-Ukraine fonts

The font files in this directory are the official `e-Ukraine` and
`e-Ukraine Head` families published by the Ministry of Digital Transformation
of Ukraine.

- Source: https://thedigital.gov.ua/fonts
- Official announcement: https://thedigital.gov.ua/news/technologies/diia_design
- Typeface designer: Dmytro Rastvortsev
- Original archives: `e-Ukraine.zip` and `e-Ukraine-Head.zip`

The files are kept unmodified from the official archives. The source website
publishes its content under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
license unless stated otherwise.
